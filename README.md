# Commercial Insurance Policy Comparison & Risk Insights Platform

**Insurance | Policy Comparison | Risk Insights | Broker Advisory Operations**

---

## Overview

An independent Business Analysis case study focused on designing a centralized platform for commercial insurance brokers to compare policy quotations from multiple insurers.

The solution addresses manual policy review, inconsistent insurer terminology, coverage-gap identification, critical exclusion analysis, recommendation workflows, audit traceability, and operational analytics.

---

## My Role

**Independent Business Analyst**

I worked across the end-to-end requirements lifecycle — from business problem analysis and process design through functional requirements, business rules, solution design, data modelling, API specifications, user stories, UAT, and analytics.

---

## The Problem

Commercial insurance brokers often compare multiple policy documents manually using document review and spreadsheets.

Key challenges included:

- Manual review of lengthy policy documents
- Different terminology across insurers
- Spreadsheet-based policy comparison
- Manual identification of coverage gaps and critical exclusions
- Limited comparison-status visibility
- Manual recommendation preparation
- Limited audit traceability

---

## What I Designed

A structured policy comparison workflow covering:

**Policy Upload → Clause Extraction → Standardization → Policy Comparison → Coverage & Exclusion Analysis → Broker Review & Override → Recommendation → Client Review → Audit Trail**

Key capabilities include:

- Policy document upload
- Clause extraction and standardization
- Pairwise policy comparison
- Coverage-gap analysis
- Critical exclusion flagging
- Broker review and manual override with justification
- Recommendation versioning
- Status tracking and completion notification
- Advisory audit trail

The solution keeps the **broker responsible for final advisory decisions**.

## Data & Analytics

Created an illustrative PostgreSQL analytics model using synthetic insurance data.

The analysis covers:

- Policy comparison turnaround
- Coverage & exclusion density
- Advisory audit activity
- Broker productivity
- Recommendation turnaround

A Tableau dashboard was created to visualize comparison turnaround, recommendation turnaround, broker activity, critical exclusions, exclusion density, and audit activity.

**All datasets and results are illustrative portfolio data, not production insurance data.**

---

## Outcome

The proposed solution is designed to:

- Reduce manual policy comparison effort
- Standardize comparison across insurer documents
- Improve visibility into coverage gaps and critical exclusions
- Improve broker turnaround visibility
- Provide structured recommendation workflows
- Strengthen auditability of advisory activities
